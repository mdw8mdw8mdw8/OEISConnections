How to Play

Find groups of four number sequences that share a common OEIS (On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences) property.

  • Select four numbers and tap Submit to check if your guess is correct.
  • You have 4 mistakes available. Repeated guesses will not count against you.
  • If you get 3 out of 4 numbers from the same sequence, you will see a "One away..." hint.
  • Beware: In some cases a number may be part of more than one sequence,
  • but there is only one correct sequence it is part of.
  • Tap any sequence name on a ribbon to view it on the OEIS website.
